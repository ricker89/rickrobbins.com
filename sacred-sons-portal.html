Member Experience

Your Profile. Your Pathway. Your Record.

Every member gets a public profile with earned rank badges, a visible growth pathway, service hour tracking, and a digital wallet. All computed from real data, not manually assigned.

Profile
Public Profiles with Earned Badges
Every member gets a vanity URL. The honeycomb of rank badges is computed from completed trainings. LT1, LT2, and Founders Circle patches light up as they are earned. Fields edit inline, and the same badge logic drives the directory, rosters, and admin views.
Pathway
A Visual Map of Growth
Members see exactly where they are in their journey and what's next. Each node represents a completed training, course, or milestone. The pathway is personal and dynamic, built from their actual participation data.
Wallet
Points, Credits, and Rewards
An internal points economy that tracks service hours, referrals, and engagement. Credits can be applied to future events and trainings. Everything is transparent and auditable.
Learning Platform

Courses, Quizzes, and Journals

Eight gated courses with signed video streaming, server-graded quizzes, and journal submissions reviewed by facilitators. All progress is tracked and feeds the member's pathway.

Catalog
Course Storefront
Every digital course, from the seven-day starter to the pillar courses. Prices pulled from the same catalog that feeds checkout. Each card leads to a dedicated sales page and instant-access purchase.
Course Reader
Gated Content with Video
Each course features a framed reader with signed Cloudflare Stream video. Content is gated by enrollment and progress. Quizzes are graded server-side, and completion unlocks the next module.
Community

Directory, Chat, and Global Map

A living community platform with real-time presence, searchable profiles, private messaging, discussion forums, and a global map showing members and events worldwide.

Directory
Searchable Member Directory
Grouped by role and rank with live "online now" presence. Filters cover location, rank, and training history. Visibility rules come from database row-level security, so members only see what they are permitted to see.
Map
Global Member and Event Map
A Mapbox-powered world map showing members and events across four continents. Toggle between member view and event view. Find your nearest circle, event, or fellow member instantly.
Chat
Real-Time Messaging
Every cohort automatically gets its own private chat room alongside its Discourse forum, Zoom series, and calendar invites. Real-time messaging powered by Supabase Realtime.
Events and Calls

Calendar, Registration, and Cohorts

Over 100 events a year across North America, Europe, and Latin America. The portal handles the full lifecycle: discovery, registration, payment, rosters, room assignments, and attendance.

Calendar
Public Event Calendar
Cards, list, month grid, or map view. Filters cover audience, format, and region. Scarcity ribbons ("2 spots left") are calculated from real ticket capacity. Public, indexed by search engines, and feeds the AI sales assistant.
Event Detail
Rich Event Pages
Each event gets a full page with facilitator bios, location details, ticket tiers, and Stripe checkout built at request time. Webhooks grant access automatically upon purchase.
Cohorts
Automated Pod Management
Every online cohort automatically gets its own Zoom series, Google Calendar invites, a private Discourse board, and a real-time chat room. A live progress grid shows modules, call attendance, and quiz results for each member.
Operations

Admin Tools That Actually Work

Role-based admin with audit logging, roster management, room assignments, journal review, event creation, and "view as member" impersonation for support. Everything staff needs in one place.

Rosters
Live Event Rosters
Synced from WooCommerce every hour. Staff can view attendance history, dietary needs, emergency contacts, and special requirements. Export to CSV for offline use at remote event locations.
Rooms
Drag-and-Drop Room Assignments
A visual board for assigning beds at retreat venues. Drag members between rooms, track capacity, and handle special accommodation requests. Automatically generates room lists for venue staff.
Journals
Facilitator Journal Review
726 journal submissions from members completing course work. Facilitators review inline, leave feedback, and mark completion. All tracked and auditable in the admin dashboard.
Landing Pages

Conversion-Optimized Pages

Public-facing pages designed to convert visitors into members. Each page is SEO-optimized with custom meta, structured data, and scarcity-driven CTAs.

Homepage
The Front Door
The public homepage drives traffic to events, trainings, and the community. Dynamic content pulls from the event calendar and course catalog so the page is always current without manual updates.
Immersion
Event Sales Pages
Dedicated landing pages for flagship events with video, testimonials, facilitator bios, and embedded Stripe checkout. Each page is a complete sales funnel in itself.
Technical Foundation

Built to Scale

57 database tables with row-level security throughout. 128 server endpoints. 1,229 commits in five months. Every purchase, roster change, call recording, and journal entry flows to the right place automatically.
